Common questions

Where is my ClearQuit data stored?

ClearQuit is designed as a local-first application. Your quit history, journal entries, cravings, lapses, and related ClearQuit information are stored on your Android device.

How do I create a backup?

Open ClearQuit and go to Profile → Data & Privacy → Create Backup. ClearQuit creates an encrypted backup file that you can save in a location you choose.

How do I restore a backup?

Open Profile → Data & Privacy → Restore Backup and select your ClearQuit backup file. You will need the password that was used when the backup was created.

I forgot my backup password. Can ClearQuit recover it?

No. ClearQuit does not store your backup password. An encrypted backup cannot normally be restored without the correct password.

How do I delete my ClearQuit data?

Open Profile → Data & Privacy → Delete All Data.

Backup files that you previously exported are separate files and must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Check ClearQuit's notification settings in Profile and make sure Android has granted notification permission to ClearQuit.
