ClearQuit Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Privacy-first design: ClearQuit is designed to work locally on your Android device. ClearQuit does not require an account and currently does not operate a remote backend that receives your quit history, journal entries, cravings, or other personal ClearQuit data.

1. About ClearQuit

ClearQuit is an Android application designed to support people who are working to stop smoking. The app provides features such as quit progress tracking, craving-management tools, personal reasons for quitting, lapse and restart tracking, journal entries, progress insights, local reminders, and encrypted backup and restore.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClearQuit accesses and stores, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information stored by ClearQuit

Depending on the features you use, ClearQuit may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Your quit date and quit-attempt history
  • Your previous smoking pattern, such as cigarettes per day and pack size
  • Pack price and currency information used to estimate money saved
  • Your personal reasons for quitting
  • Triggers you choose to record
  • Craving intensity and Rescue-session information
  • Coping methods you choose during Rescue sessions
  • Lapse or restart records and optional notes
  • Journal entries
  • Mood and optional craving-level check-ins
  • Journal tags
  • Support contacts that you choose to enter
  • Notification and reminder preferences
  • Progress and milestone information derived from the data above

3. How your information is used

ClearQuit uses information stored on your device to provide the features you request. For example, the app may use your quit date and previous smoking pattern to calculate elapsed quit time, cigarettes avoided, packs avoided, estimated money saved, and progress milestones.

Your recorded cravings, triggers, coping methods, journal entries, moods, and lapse history may be used locally to show your personal history and insights inside ClearQuit.

4. Local storage and data transmission

ClearQuit is currently designed as a local-first application. The app does not require a ClearQuit account.

ClearQuit currently has no ClearQuit-operated server or cloud backend for storing your quit history, journal entries, craving records, lapse records, or other ClearQuit history.

The Android application currently does not request Internet access for its normal ClearQuit functionality.

ClearQuit does not currently include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs that receive your ClearQuit history.

5. Journal, craving, and lapse information

Journal entries, craving information, triggers, lapse notes, and similar records can contain personal information. These records are stored locally in the application's private storage on your device.

ClearQuit does not intentionally include your journal text, lapse notes, craving notes, or trigger notes in notification content.

6. Notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, ClearQuit may provide local reminders such as daily check-in reminders, quit-progress milestones, and optional reminders about your reason for quitting.

Notification permission is optional. ClearQuit remains usable if you do not grant notification permission.

Your personal reason for quitting is not displayed in notification content unless you explicitly enable that option in ClearQuit.

Notification settings are stored locally on your device.

7. Backup and restore

ClearQuit allows you to create a portable backup of your ClearQuit history.

Backups are created only when you request them and choose where to save the backup file using Android's document-storage interface.

ClearQuit backups are encrypted using a password that you choose. The backup system uses password-based key derivation and authenticated encryption to protect the contents of the backup.

ClearQuit does not store your backup password for you. If you lose the password, ClearQuit may not be able to restore that encrypted backup.

Because you choose where to save or copy a backup file, the privacy and security of that file can also depend on the storage provider or location you choose.

Notification permission, notification scheduling state, and device-specific notification settings are not currently included in ClearQuit backup files.

8. Data sharing

ClearQuit does not currently sell your personal ClearQuit data.

ClearQuit does not currently provide your journal entries, craving records, lapse records, quit history, or personal reasons to advertisers.

ClearQuit does not currently use advertising networks.

9. Data security

ClearQuit stores its working application data within Android's application-private storage.

User-created portable backup files are encrypted before they are written to the location you select.

No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. You should protect access to your device and protect the password used for your ClearQuit backups.

10. Data retention

ClearQuit data generally remains stored locally on your device until you edit it, delete individual records where that feature is available, use ClearQuit's delete-all-data function, clear the application's storage through Android, or uninstall the application.

Backup files that you create remain wherever you chose to save them until you delete them from that storage location yourself.

11. Deleting your data

ClearQuit provides a delete-all-data option inside the app.

Using this option removes ClearQuit's locally stored user data and preferences from the application and returns ClearQuit to its initial onboarding state.

ClearQuit also cancels its scheduled reminders and clears its local notification state as part of this reset.

Backup files that you previously exported are separate files and are not automatically deleted by resetting ClearQuit. You must delete those files from the location where you saved them if you no longer want to retain them.

12. Android permissions

ClearQuit currently requests the Android notification permission only if you choose to enable notification features on Android versions where that permission is required.

Notification permission is optional and may be changed using Android system settings.

13. Health and wellness information

ClearQuit is intended to support personal smoking-cessation tracking and reflection.

ClearQuit is not intended to diagnose a medical condition, provide emergency medical services, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

If you have questions about your health, medications, withdrawal symptoms, or treatment options, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.

14. Children's privacy

ClearQuit does not currently operate an account system or server that intentionally collects children's personal information.

Age eligibility and target-audience information for distribution may also be governed by the requirements of the app store through which ClearQuit is distributed.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ClearQuit's features or data practices change.

When the policy is updated, the effective date shown at the top of this page will also be updated.

16. Privacy questions and support

ClearQuit users may submit privacy or support inquiries through the project's public GitHub issue page:

https://github.com/anishlr/clearquit-privacy/issues

Because GitHub issues are public, please do not include journal entries, health details, passwords, backup files, or other sensitive personal information in an issue.

A dedicated privacy/support contact may be added here in the future.
